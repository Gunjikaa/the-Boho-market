Our Story

Rooted in Creativity, Woven with Intention

The Boho Market was born from a love for free-spirited living, handcrafted treasures, and the artists who pour their souls into every piece.

We partner with independent artisans across India who use traditional techniques — block printing, macrame, natural dyeing — to create pieces that tell a story. Each purchase supports fair wages and keeps ancient crafts alive.
